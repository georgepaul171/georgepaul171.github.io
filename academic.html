Coursework projects

Reinforcement Learning with PPO

2025
MSc team project · University of Bath

The problem. Teaching an agent effective long-horizon behaviour from sparse rewards is hard: small design choices in representation and reward shape strategy in ways that are easy to get wrong.

The approach. We trained a PPO agent in a boxing environment. I focused on the feature extractor and on how reward shaping changed long-term play. It was a useful counterweight to my Bayesian work in thinking about sequential decisions under uncertainty.

What I took from it. The project reinforced how much evaluation and ablation matter when comparing algorithmic choices, a theme that carries into my applied evaluation work.
